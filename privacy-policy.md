## Overview
MultiChat AI is a browser extension that helps you chat with multiple AI websites simultaneously within a single browser tab. We take your privacy seriously.

## Data Collection
MultiChat AI does NOT collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. The extension operates entirely within your browser. No analytics, telemetry, or tracking code is included. No crash reports are collected.

## How It Works
The extension loads AI websites inside iframes within a dashboard page. When you type a question, your message is sent to each AI via the browser's built-in messaging system. Each AI processes your question on its own servers per its own privacy policy. MultiChat AI itself never sees, intercepts, stores, or transmits your conversations.

## Permissions
| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| storage | Save preferences locally |
| scripting | Inject scripts for message passing |
| declarativeNetRequest | Strip iframe-blocking headers |
| activeTab | Open dashboard on icon click |
| tabs | Manage extension tab |
| contextMenus | Lock Send Button |
| host: all_urls | Support any user-added AI site |

All data stays local. Nothing uploaded or shared.

## Third-Party Services
When you interact with AI services, those services process messages per their own policies: DeepSeek, Kimi, Qianwen, Yuanbao, Doubao.

## Your Data
All settings stored via chrome.storage.local. Clear by removing the extension.
